# Mô tả kênh — Learn Something Daily

Dán vào YouTube Studio → Tùy chỉnh → Thông tin cơ bản → Mô tả (tối đa 1 000 ký tự; bản dưới ≈ 890 ký tự).

Why do you read the same paragraph three times? Is "8 glasses of water a day" real? What does your brain do all night?

Learn Something Daily turns everyday questions into short science mysteries. Each video starts with a story you'll recognize, gives you the clues, and pauses so you can guess the answer before we reveal it.

What you can expect:
• Real science, checked against the original studies, with every source listed in the description
• Myths busted — and honest notes when the evidence is still debated
• Clear animated explanations, about 8–10 minutes each
• English subtitles on every video

Topics: the human body, the brain, sleep, food and water, focus, and the small mysteries of daily life.

Subscribe and learn one new thing at a time.

Our videos are for general education only and are not medical advice.
Illustrations are AI-generated; narration uses an AI voice.
