## Description
You spend about a third of your life asleep. Is it wasted time, or is your brain working the night shift? Collect the clues, pause, and guess before the answer!

Inspired by Matthew Walker's book *Why We Sleep* (2017). Every number in this video comes from the original studies below, not from the book.
