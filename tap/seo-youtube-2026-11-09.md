**Title:** How Much Water Do You Really Need? The 8 Glasses a Day Myth

**Description**
```
How much water do you really need? About 60% of your body is water, and it's replaced every day. Too little leaves you foggy and tired; too much can be dangerous. So should you drink exactly 8 glasses, as much as possible, or just when you're thirsty? Pause and guess before the answer!

What water does for you
• Too little: losing just 1.4% of body weight in water brings headaches, tiredness and poor focus
• Too much, too fast: it can dangerously dilute the sodium in your blood
• No magic "8 glasses": drink when thirsty, and more when it's hot, when you exercise or when you're sick
• Pale straw-yellow pee means you're well hydrated
```
